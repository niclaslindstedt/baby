type: Added
title: Your child's usual times, one tap away
doc: sleep
---

Once a few days are logged, the "when?" sheet offers your child's usual time under **Now**: **Usual bedtime** when you log last night the next morning, **After a usual nap** for a wake that was never tapped, and **Usual morning** for a night's. Tapping Nap or Night within a minute of Woke up now carries on the same sleep instead of starting a new one.
