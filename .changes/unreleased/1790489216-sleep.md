Log naps and nights with one tap to start and one to end, from the new Sleep tab or the + on any screen — as they happen, or afterwards by picking how long ago or a time on the clock. Today shows whether your child is asleep or awake, last night and the month's average, and suggests when the next nap or bedtime is likely to suit from the last sleep, its length and your child's own rhythm; its view has the 30- and 90-day averages against the WHO recommendation for the age, two weeks as a chart and the week hour by hour.
