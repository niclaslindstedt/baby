title: The phone app keeps the record on this device
doc: native-app
---

The iPhone and Android app keeps your child's record on the phone and offers nowhere else to keep it: Settings has no section for where the record lives, so there is nothing to connect and no passphrase to keep, and Your data says the record stays on this device; export a backup to keep a copy of your own. Settings → About and the About screen say so — nothing is sent when the app opens, and nothing goes to iCloud or any other cloud — and link to the full privacy policy. If the device refuses to save, the message points at its storage space. The website and the desktop app keep their other places, unchanged.
