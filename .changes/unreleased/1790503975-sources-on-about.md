**Settings → About and sources** lists the published guidelines, studies and health service pages the app's numbers come from, the strongest evidence first — each with a line on what it is used for, the words the numbers were taken from, and a link to the source. It works offline; the link is the only thing that leaves the app, and only when you tap it. [Learn more](feature:sources)
