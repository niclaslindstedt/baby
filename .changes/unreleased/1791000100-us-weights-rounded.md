On a US phone a reading's weight now reads to the whole ounce ("17 lb 11 oz", not "17 lb 11.2 oz"), and the forecast to the nearest quarter pound ("about 20 lb (likely 18½ lb–21¾ lb)"), which is as precise as a forecast months out can honestly be. What you typed is kept exactly as before.
