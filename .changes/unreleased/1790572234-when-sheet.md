title: Diapers and sleeps ask "when?" after the tap
doc: sleep
---

Tapping Pee, Poop, Both, Nap, Night or Woke up now opens a small sheet: a big **Now**, tiles for 5 minutes to an hour ago that show the time each stands for, and **Another time** on the 24-hour dial for anything further back — so a diaper logged after the baby is dressed again gets its real time too. The row of time chips above the sleep buttons is gone, and times that can't be right, like waking before the sleep began, are dimmed instead of refused after the tap.
