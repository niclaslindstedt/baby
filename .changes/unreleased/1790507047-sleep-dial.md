The Sleep tab opens on a 24-hour dial with the last day's sleeps drawn round it and the sleep — or the time awake — counting up to the second, and a sleep logged late is put right by dragging its ends round the dial, the way a bedtime alarm is set, instead of typing dates and times. Today's Sleep card runs the clock too, with a ring filling toward the suggested next nap and a countdown to it. [Learn more](feature:sleep)
