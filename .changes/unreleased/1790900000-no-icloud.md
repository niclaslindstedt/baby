type: Changed
title: Your child's record never goes to iCloud
doc: native-app
---

The phone app no longer offers iCloud Drive. Apple's guideline 5.1.3(ii) keeps personal health information out of iCloud, and this is your child's: it stays on your device, or in your own Dropbox if you connect it. The phone app has not been released yet, so there is nothing to move; a pre-release install that had chosen iCloud Drive comes back to this device.
