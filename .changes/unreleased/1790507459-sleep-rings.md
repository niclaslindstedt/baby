Sleep fills a ring as your child sleeps — the night and the naps in their own colours, closing at the top of the range the WHO recommends for the age, with that range marked on the track. Today's Sleep card carries the last 24 hours this way, and its view draws them large beside the 30- and 90-day averages on rings of their own.
