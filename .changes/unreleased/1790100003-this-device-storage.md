type: Changed
title: One "This device" instead of two
doc: cloud-sync
---

"Where the record lives" listed **This device** — which kept no second copy — beside **IndexedDB**. They were the same device, so they are one choice now, backed by IndexedDB; an older install moves across on its own with nothing to do and nothing lost. Where the browser has no directory picker, Settings now says why **Local folder** isn't offered instead of leaving it out silently. [Learn more](feature:cloud-sync)
