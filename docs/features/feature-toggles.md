# What you track

Not everyone tracks everything. A formula-fed newborn has no food regimen for
months; some families write diapers on the fridge; someone may have installed
the app for the vaccination card alone. **Settings → What you track** has a
switch for each of the four trackers — diapers, growth, food and vaccines —
and everything is on until you say otherwise.

Switch one off and it leaves the app: its tab disappears from the bottom bar,
the swipe closes over the gap, and its card on Today goes with it. Switching
off **Diapers** also takes the **+** out of the top bar and the one-tap
buttons off Today, because that tracker's whole surface is those buttons.

Today always stays. It is the home screen, it is where the app lands when a
tab disappears under you, and with all four trackers off it still tells you
how old your child is.

**Nothing is deleted.** The switches decide what is on screen and nothing
else: every reading, diaper change, food and vaccination stays in the record,
keeps syncing, and is still in your backups. Switch a tracker back on months
later and it is exactly where you left it.

The choice is made per device, alongside the theme and the language, rather
than in the record itself — so the phone in your pocket can show the diaper
buttons while the tablet in the kitchen shows only the food regimen.
