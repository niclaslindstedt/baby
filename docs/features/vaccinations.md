# Vaccinations

The Swedish childhood vaccination programme as a timeline for your child:
every dose Folkhälsomyndigheten's programme gives, at the age it is given —
rotavirus at six weeks, the three-, five- and twelve-month visits, MPR at
eighteen months, the five-year booster, and the school doses — marked as
given, expected by now, or upcoming, with the expected date counted from
the birth date.

Tap **Mark as given** to record a dose with its date and, if you like, the
vaccine's name off the card and a note. Vaccinations outside the general
programme — BCG, the hepatitis B birth dose, the RSV antibody, influenza,
chickenpox, TBE, meningococcal, an extra pneumococcal dose, or anything else
— are listed separately so the card stays complete without muddling the
timeline.

Today's **Next vaccination** card opens the card at a glance: every visit the
programme books, in order, with a tick against what you have recorded and a
count of how much of the card is done. It is an overview and nothing more —
the route, the notes, the product names and the extras stay on the Vaccines
tab, where they are entered.

The ages are the programme's; the child health centre calls you in and may
adjust them. The programme and its sources are in
[the vaccinations documentation](../vaccinations.md).
