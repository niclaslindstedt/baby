# Today

The first screen, and the reason the app is opened: your child's age, the
three diaper buttons, and one line each on the other three questions the app
answers.

Tap **Pee**, **Poo** or **Both** when you change a diaper. The time is
recorded for you, the last 24 hours count up as wet and dirty, and today's
changes are listed underneath so a mistap is one tap to remove. When the
count looks thin for your child's age, Today says so — see
[diapers](feature:diapers).

Under that, a week of diapers as a chart, and three cards: whether the food
regimen still covers the day ([food](feature:food)), the latest growth
reading and its trend ([growth](feature:growth)), and the next vaccination
([vaccinations](feature:vaccinations)). Each opens its screen. The point of
gathering them here is that a parent with fifteen seconds gets the whole
picture without visiting four tabs.
