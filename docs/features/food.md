# Food

Before about six months there is nothing to track — breast milk or formula
is everything your baby needs, and the Food screen says so and asks only how
the milk is fed.

From six months, keep a **daily regimen**: the foods your child typically
gets in a day, each with a daily amount. It is not a diary. Only calories are
needed for each food; iron, vitamin D and the fats are entered when a label
says so, and a blank is treated as unknown, never as zero. The **Common
foods** chips fill in typical values for porridge, välling, egg, salmon,
banana, avocado, rapeseed oil, infant formula, tillskottsnäring and the milk
drinks sold from a year.

The screen then answers the app's central question: **does the regimen cover
the day?** Energy first — against the recommendation for your child's age
and latest weight, with breast milk's expected share left out of the target
because it cannot be measured — then iron, vitamin D, the fat shares,
omega-3 and omega-6, and DHA, against the Nordic Nutrition Recommendations 2023.

## Milk

Say how the milk is fed, and on bottles say **which formula**: infant formula
(modersmjölksersättning) or follow-on formula (tillskottsnäring, sold from six
months). They are not brand variants — tillskottsnäring carries about two and
a half times the iron, which is the reason it exists, so the answer moves the
iron figure on this screen. Infant formula is fine for the whole first year,
so say what your baby actually gets rather than what the age suggests.

For a baby who gets **both** breast and bottle, the formula is counted as
milk replacing breast milk rather than as extra on top: adding formula raises
what the day is measured against by exactly what the bottles bring, so the
bottles never cover the day on their own. Breast milk itself is still not
measured — nothing it contributes is counted in the nutrients below, which
for iron changes little, since breast milk carries almost none.

As your child grows the target moves and the regimen does not. When a
regimen that used to be enough no longer is, the screen says so in one
sentence, and you increase a portion, add a food or change the regimen until
it covers the day again. The targets and their sources are in
[the nutrition documentation](../nutrition.md).
